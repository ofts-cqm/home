# What do you think about AI?

OK here is just my personal thoughts about AI. Don't take it too series. I am not making a claim, I am not trying to teach you something. It is just my experience with AI, nothing more. 

AI is the greatest tool ever invented; with the help of AI, I gain unimaginable productivity. I have to be honest, without AI, I lost most of my efficiency, but with the help of AI, I can vibe-code or semi-vibe-code comprehensive systems and software. 

## Do you like to use AI?

Actually, no... which may surprise you. This is my honest answer. You see, I study software engineering because coding is an enjoyable thing. If it's not enjoyable, why would I choose this major? But, to me, the process of coding is what is enjoyable. The outcome, the result... not really important. 

There are many reasons why people choose to study software. I know someone study coding because they like new stuff; I know someone study coding because they like to automate everything. I am not, I simply loves to communicate with computer, not through a chat window, but through codes, thousands lines of codes. 

Coding, debugging, sitting in front of the computer wondering why this doesn't work, and sitting in front of the computer celebrating it finally works, are all processes of coding, which I consider to be enjoyable, even debugging for hours.

As you can see, AI destroys this feeling. Coding is like a video game, the enjoyable part is not the final "Thank you for playing" screen, the enjoyable part is the process itself. If there is a button that skips the entire process and jump directly to the final scene, would you press that button? You won't.

If I made a project, I get a sense of pride and a sense of achievement. If I let AI do it, I get nothing, because this is literally other people's work. Sometimes I struggle about this. If I don't use AI, then I lose productivity. If I use AI, then I lose fun.

## Then, are you still OK with using AI in work?

AI is the best tool ever invented. I am a bit nostogic, I am not stupid. These AI Miracles are enough to change my minds. It is the future of work, the future of software engineering, and probably the future of every Industries. 

I am still very agile, able to adopt to new technology. I know exactly how to use AI, how to use them efficiently, and how to use them productively. I know about agents, about these fancy skills and mcp servers, I can configure my AI agents to fit my workflow. Maybe a few years later, when I look back, I will laugh at me and say "look how stupid this guy is, he think AI replaces his fun stuff."
