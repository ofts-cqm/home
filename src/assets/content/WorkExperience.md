My first and only work experience is [Ascendance Foundry](https://www.ascendancefoundry.com).

Ascendance Foundry is a consulting firm that brings automation to various industries. Ascendance Foundry sends out "Forward Deployed Engineers" to various industries to help them integrate AI into their existing workflow to boost productivity. Now as agentic coding is wide spreading, it significantly reduces the cost of making customized softwares affordable to smalal-sized companies. However, this also requires developer's dicipline to create maintainble and scalable softwares.

In Ascendance Foundry, I worked for an private equity firm to automate their accounting process. Utilizing their Microsoft stack, by connecting to different platforms, I am able to automate processes including but not limited to **payroll processing**, **expense recharging**, **financial statement generating**, and **managing deals' transactions**. 

With the help of AI, plus my knowledge learned from Unversity, I am able to compress these processes that used to take a few days down to just one click, while significantly reducing bookkeeper error rate. This is because human's errors compounds. Even if you can do 95% of your journals correct, well, a month's deal contains 29 journals to post, that brings the posibility down to 0.95^29 = 22.5%. With the accounting automation, because data are all computed from well configured tables and are automatically validated based on rule set, that brings the error rate down to zero.
